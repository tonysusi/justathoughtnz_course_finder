# Course Catalogue

## Generalised Anxiety

An online CBT course for people experiencing broad, persistent worry that interferes with relationships, work, daily life or leisure. It teaches skills for managing thoughts, reducing anxiety and physical symptoms, facing fears and preventing relapse.

## Depression

An online CBT course for people experiencing sadness, loss of interest, poor concentration or feelings of worthlessness. It teaches practical skills to understand and reduce depression symptoms and support lasting recovery.

## Mixed Depression & Anxiety

An online CBT course for people experiencing low mood alongside anxiety or nervousness. It combines skills for managing sadness, worry, hopelessness and low self-worth, supporting both immediate improvement and longer-term wellbeing.

## Social Anxiety

An online CBT course for people who worry about what others think in social situations or avoid social experiences. It explains social anxiety and teaches practical skills for tackling it.

## Overcoming Panic

A course for understanding how fear of panic attacks and avoidance can create a difficult cycle. It teaches practical strategies for tackling panic and regaining confidence in everyday life.

## Overcoming Panic – Next Steps

A follow-on course for people who have completed Overcoming Panic. It helps troubleshoot common roadblocks and setbacks and strengthens CBT skills for managing panic and anxiety.

## Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

An online CBT course explaining unwanted obsessive thoughts and the repetitive behaviours or mental routines used to relieve them. It teaches practical, evidence-based skills for tackling OCD and responding differently to distressing thoughts.

## Health Anxiety

A course for people whose health worries lead to symptom searching, body checking, reassurance seeking or avoidance. It teaches practical ways to reduce health-related anxiety and its effect on wellbeing, whether or not a medical condition has been diagnosed.

## Staying on Track

A short wellbeing course offering practical strategies for coping with stress and uncertainty. It supports mental and emotional health and helps participants and their whānau put useful self-care skills into practice.

## Managing Insomnia

A course for people whose difficulty falling or staying asleep is affecting their energy, mood, concentration or daily life. It teaches practical skills for changing unhelpful sleep patterns, reducing sleep-related worry and getting sleep back on track.

## Just a Pause – Intro to Mindfulness

A four-lesson introduction to mindfulness for people dealing with worry, stress or feeling overwhelmed. It teaches practices for becoming calmer, more grounded and connected to the present moment.

## Coping with Stress

An online CBT course for people feeling overwhelmed or stuck in stress. It teaches practical skills for understanding stress, responding to it more effectively and beginning to feel better.

## Thinking About Change

An alcohol and drug support course for people concerned about their substance use but unsure whether they want or need to change. It helps participants reflect on their use and its effects, clarify what matters to them and consider their options.

## Taking Action

An alcohol and drug support course for people ready to change their substance use or maintain changes already made. Practical strategies and guided exercises help participants navigate change, understand influential thoughts and emotions and plan for the future.

## Getting Back on Track

An alcohol and drug support course for people finding it difficult to maintain previous changes. It teaches practical ways to respond to a lapse or relapse, identify what is needed and take positive steps towards lasting change.

## Pregnancy Wellbeing

A course for people experiencing persistent anxiety, depression or low mood during pregnancy. Developed with clinical and lived-experience input, it teaches practical skills for supporting mental health and wellbeing while preparing for a growing whānau.

## Postnatal Wellbeing

A course for people experiencing anxiety or depression after childbirth. It teaches practical CBT-based skills for improving wellbeing and supporting recovery while caring for a new baby and growing whānau.

## Staying on Track (NZ Sign Language)

An NZSL version of the short wellbeing course, offering easy-to-use strategies for coping with stress and difficult or uncertain situations. It supports mental and emotional health and helps participants and their whānau stay on track.

## Mixed Depression & Anxiety (NZ Sign Language)

An NZSL-accessible online CBT course for people experiencing low mood together with anxiety or nervousness. It combines skills for managing sadness, worry, hopelessness and low self-worth.
